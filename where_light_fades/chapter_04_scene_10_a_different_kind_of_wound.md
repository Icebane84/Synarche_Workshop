# Chapter 4, Scene 10: A Different Kind of Wound

**ID:** `narr-act-02-ch-04-sc-10`
**Version:** v1.0
**Scene Type:** Core Narrative Prose

---

The silence from Kaelen's chamber was a heavier burden than any argument. Garrett had retreated into the cold comfort of his maps and ledgers, his anger a whetstone he used to sharpen his focus. But Serafina found no such solace. Her empathy, usually a source of strength, was now a raw, open wound. Kaelen's terror felt like her own.

She found Brother Caius in the Great Library, in a small, secluded alcove where he painstakingly restored ancient, water-damaged scrolls. The air smelled of old parchment and the faint, sweet scent of binding glue. He looked up as she approached, his kind, weary eyes seeing the storm in her before she spoke a word.

"He has built a wall, Brother Caius," she said, her voice barely a whisper, afraid the towering shelves themselves might be listening. "A prison. And I cannot find the gate."

Caius set down his delicate brush, his movements slow and deliberate. He did not feign ignorance. "Some prisons, my child, are not built to keep the world out. They are built to keep the monster in."

"He is not a monster," Serafina insisted, a flash of her usual fire returning.

"No," Caius agreed, his voice full of a profound, ancient sorrow. "He is a good man who has been handed a monstrous weapon. And he fears, rightly, that the longer he holds it, the more his hand will begin to resemble it."

He gestured to a massive, leather-bound tome resting on a nearby lectern, its pages open to an illustration of a Paladin kneeling before a shattered sword. "The Order teaches us to fight the darkness. We train our bodies to be shields and our swords to be flames. But we have no doctrine for a wound that is already inside the blade. We have no prayer for a poison that has seeped into the very steel we trust."

Serafina looked at the illustration, at the Paladin's despair, and felt a chill of recognition. "What can I do? My Light... it feels like I am trying to heal a shadow. It offers no purchase."

"Because you are trying to mend a wound of the body," Caius said, his gaze returning to her, sharp and piercing. "This is a wound of history. A wound of the soul. You cannot heal it with the Light alone." He paused, his next words chosen with immense care. "You must understand the nature of the poison. You must understand the history of the blade."

It was not the answer she had hoped for. It was not a prayer, not a ritual, not a path to healing. It was a task. An investigation. A different kind of burden.

Serafina looked from Caius's sorrowful face to the endless, silent shelves of the library. Garrett was hunting for a conspiracy in ledgers and timelines. Kaelen was lost in a prison of his own making. And she... she would have to hunt for an answer in the one place she had never thought to look: not in the heart of her friend, but in the haunted history of the weapon that was tearing him apart.
She didn't even know if healing was possible.
