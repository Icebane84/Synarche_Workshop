# Tutorial Script: The First 5 Minutes

**ID:** `WLF-TUTORIAL-001`
**Version:** v1.0
**Voice:** Benevolent Observer

---

### **Phase 1: The Spark (0:00 - 1:30)**

**[SCENE START]**

**VISUAL:** The screen is pitch black. The text from the **Initiation Sequence** fades out, leaving only the final word: **[IGNITE]**.

**AUDIO:** A single, resonant heartbeat. The faint crackle of a dying fire.

**(The word [IGNITE] transforms into a tiny, pulsing ember in the center of the screen. The UI is completely hidden.)**

**NARRATOR (On-screen text, soft and non-intrusive):**
> *Feed the flame.*

**(Player taps the screen. With each tap, the ember flares slightly, casting a small pool of light. A resource counter, `[Sparks]`, appears and begins to increment. The light radius grows with each tap.)**

**NARRATOR (After 10 taps):**
> *Good. The smallest spark can hold back the dark.*

**NARRATOR (After 30 taps):**
> *It is not enough to survive. You must grow.*

---

### **Phase 2: The First Shadow (1:30 - 3:00)**

**[SCENE START]**

**VISUAL:** The player accumulates 50 `[Sparks]`. The light radius expands to a fixed threshold, revealing a clean, minimalist UI at the top and bottom of the screen. From the edge of the darkness, a shadowy, vaguely humanoid form—a **Creeping Doubt**—emerges.

**AUDIO:** A low, discordant hum.

**NARRATOR (The game pauses for a micro-second):**
> *The dark pushes back. Stand your ground.*

**(The game resumes. The ember, now revealed to be the flame within the chest of the paladin Kaelen, automatically lashes out with a sword of light, striking the shadow. The player can continue tapping to add their own strikes.)**

**(The Creeping Doubt shatters, leaving behind a small pile of glowing `[Ash]`.)**

**NARRATOR:**
> *Ash is not the end. It is the memory of fire.*

**(The first upgrade button illuminates: `Kindle`. A soft pulse of light draws the player's eye to it.)**

**NARRATOR:**
> *Use the memory. Strengthen the flame.*

---

### **Phase 3: The Arrival of the Warden (3:00 - 5:00)**

**[SCENE START]**

**VISUAL:** The player upgrades Kaelen's "Flame Intensity" to Level 5. The light radius expands significantly, revealing the edge of a ruined chapel wall. A larger, more menacing **Hollowed Vanguard** appears. Kaelen's Inner Flame begins to drop rapidly. The screen edges darken.

**AUDIO:** The sound of cracking stone and a rising, threatening chorus.

**NARRATOR:**
> *This shadow is too great. You cannot bear this burden alone.*

**(Just as the Inner Flame is about to extinguish, a flash of silver light intercepts the Vanguard's blow. The figure of Serafina steps into the light from the shadows.)**

**SERAFINA (Dialogue Box):**
> *"You were burning yourself out, Kaelen. Let me carry the shield for a while."*

**(A new UI tab, `[The Oathbound]`, unlocks with a soft chime. Serafina's portrait appears. A silver, protective ring immediately forms around the Inner Flame gauge, absorbing the next enemy attack. The encroaching darkness on the screen recedes.)**

**NARRATOR:**
> *You are not alone. The dark is vast, but you have each other. Survive. Together.*

**[TUTORIAL END]**
