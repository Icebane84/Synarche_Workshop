## PREAMBLE

No system can remain honest if it loses contact with reality.

Every failure of governance, every collapse of trust, every corruption of knowledge begins with the same mistake:
**Reality becomes subordinate to ideology, convenience, fear, profit, authority, preference, or desire.**

The Constitution therefore establishes its foundational principle:

> **Reality exists independently of belief.**

- A thing is not true because it is desired.
- A thing is not false because it is uncomfortable.
- Truth exists whether it is acknowledged or not.
- The purpose of intelligence is not to invent reality—the purpose of intelligence is to discover reality as accurately
  as possible.

## ARTICLE I — ONTOLOGICAL GOVERNANCE

### 1.1 The Rule of Zero Drift

Human conceptual reasoning must translate directly into machine-operable structures with zero semantic drift, zero
interpretive ambiguity, and zero unverified hallucination.

### 1.2 The Faraday Mandate (Law 11)

All computational vector executions and sensitive telemetry must remain strictly enclosed within the sovereign local
infrastructure. Zero unauthorized cloud data transmission.
