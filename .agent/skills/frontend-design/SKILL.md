## 🏗️ SYSTEMIC PRINCIPLES

### 1. Philosophy: Pixels with Purpose

**"Every pixel has purpose. Restraint is luxury. User psychology drives decisions."**

- **Rule 1: Think, Don't Memorize.** Don't copy-paste hex codes or layout patterns (Bento, Hero Split) without proving
  the "Why."
- **Rule 2: Ask, Don't Assume.** If color, style, or layout are vague → **ASK.**
- **Rule 3: Anti-Safe-Harbor.** Avoid predictable AI clichés (Dark + Neon, Mesh Gradients, Glassmorphism, Purple
  everything).

## 🔍 ACTIONABLE CONSTRAINTS

Before any design work, answer these or **ASK USER**:

1. **Timeline**: How much time is available?
2. **Content**: Placeholder or final?
3. **Brand**: Existing guidelines?
4. **Tech**: What stack is being used?
5. **Audience**: Who exactly is the user? (Gen Z, B2B, Luxury, etc.)

### ⛔ AI TENDENCIES TO AVOID (ANTI-SAFE-HARBOR):

- **PURPLE BAN ✅**: No purple/violet backgrounds unless requested.
- **Glassmorphism**: Avoid as a default "premium" indicator.
- **Bento Grids**: Don't use for simple content that doesn't need them.
- **Mesh Gradients**: The "new" lazy background. Avoid.
